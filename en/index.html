BEYOND THE BASICS

Beauty school teaches the basics.
Salon work requires more.

Clients can feel your skill from the very first touch.

Steps and theory are an essential foundation for every facialist — and they matter. What you learned at school is the starting point.

But in the salon, clients don't judge your steps — they feel your touch. Within seconds, they can tell whether your hands are confident or unsure. The reassurance of the first contact. Palms that stay close to the skin. Pressure that moves in the right direction. Movements that flow without a break.

The same routine can feel completely different, depending on how you touch.

THE TOUCH

9 salon techniques clients can feel

Some things can't be learned from videos or manuals. You learn them through your own hands, one by one.

01

First touch

Clients feel your technique from the very first second. Learn how to place your hands, with the right warmth and speed.

02

Full-palm contact

Not just fingertips. Wrap the skin with your whole palm — the quality of contact shapes the comfort of the treatment.

03

Direction of pressure

Pressure is not only about strength, but where it goes. Learn to follow the natural flow of the skin and muscles.

04

Body mechanics

Work from your posture and whole body, not from your wrists, for stable movements with less strain.

05

Weight transfer

Create pressure by shifting your body weight, not by pushing with your hands — for deep, even, comfortable pressure.

06

Transitions

Connect one movement to the next without breaking the flow. Flow is what clients remember.

07

Hand release

How you lift your hands matters, too. Finish every movement — and the whole treatment — with care.

08

Rhythm

Not too fast, not too slow. A steady rhythm brings comfort and trust.

09

Techniques for real salons

Not techniques for the classroom, but techniques you can use in your salon work.

TECHNIQUE

Beyond the steps — the touch, in practice

Learn professional salon touch, pressure, contact, body mechanics and hand movement—not just a sequence.

01

Point makeup removal

Gently remove eye and lip makeup while minimizing strain on delicate skin.

02

Cleansing

Cleanse the face, neck and décolleté, with attention to contact, pressure and direction.

03

Cotton removal

Remove remaining cleanser carefully, without rubbing the skin.

04

Sponge removal

Use sponges suited to each area to leave the skin clean, down to the details.

05

Lotion pack

A pack step that delivers lotion evenly across the face while conditioning the skin.

06

Lotion application

Apply lotion with minimal friction, as if wrapping the skin.

07

Vinophis facial massage

One continuous flow across the face, neck and décolleté. Learn contact, pressure, direction and rhythm in detail.

08

Lifting facial massage

Work three-dimensionally while observing the balance of the whole face — face line, cheeks and eye area.

09

Finishing

Care to the very end. Condition the skin and close the treatment with a gentle touch.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is the training held?

Training is held in Japan. Training location and schedule details will be provided during the consultation/application process.

Is the training taught in English?

Please contact us in advance regarding language support and training arrangements.

Do I need to take STEP 1 to 3 in order?

No. Each step is a separate course. We recommend STEP 1 for beginners, but experienced therapists can choose only the steps they need.

Can I take all three steps in one day?

No. Each step is held as a separate course, and the three steps are not completed in a single day. Scheduling details will be provided during the consultation/application process.

I studied the basics at another school. Is this training still useful for me?

Yes. We respect the basics you have learned and build on them with the touch needed in real salons — first touch, full-palm contact, pressure direction and transitions.

I already use beauty devices. Will this help me?

Yes. This training does not teach how to operate devices. It teaches the facial salon techniques that support device treatments — including cleansing, cotton work, towel work and how you touch the skin.

Who arranges travel and accommodation?

Travel, accommodation, visa/entry requirements, and related expenses are the participant's responsibility. Please check the latest entry requirements for Japan before traveling.

Will I receive a diploma?

Yes. A diploma is awarded upon completion of each step.
